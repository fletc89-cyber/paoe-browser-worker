PAOE Social

Operated by PAOE (OccupyAI).

PAOE Social is an automated content publishing system. It manages and publishes social content on accounts that have explicitly authorised it, and only through each platform's official API.

What it does

  • Authenticates an account holder through the platform's own consent screen and acts only on accounts that grant that authorisation.
  • Produces original video and image content, and publishes it to the authorised account.
  • Reads back the publication status of content it uploaded, and the platform's own public view count for that content.
  • Stops immediately when authorisation is revoked.

What it does not do

  • It does not read private messages, and does not access content belonging to other users.
  • It does not act on any account that has not authorised it.
  • It does not buy, scrape or harvest personal data or contact lists, and it does not send bulk or unsolicited messages to strangers.
  • It does not collect the identity of any viewer.

Platforms

Publication runs through the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. When connected to a TikTok account, it uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only on accounts that have completed TikTok's own consent flow.

Authorisation and revocation

Authorisation is granted by the account holder on the platform, and may be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings. Revoking access takes effect immediately: PAOE Social can no longer authenticate and stops publishing. Contact us at fletc89@gmail.com to ask what we hold about your account, or to have it deleted.
