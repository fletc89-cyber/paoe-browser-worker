Privacy Policy

PAOE Social is operated by Autonomous Press LLC. Last updated: 2026-10-05.

This policy explains what the PAOE Social TikTok integration accesses, why, how it is stored, who it is shared with, and how it is deleted. It describes only what the integration actually does.

1. TikTok authorization

PAOE Social connects to TikTok through TikTok's official authorization (OAuth 2.0). The account owner is shown TikTok's own consent screen and grants access there; PAOE never asks for, and never receives, a TikTok password. The authorization issues an access token (and a refresh token) that PAOE stores to act on the authorized account's behalf. Access can be revoked at any time (see section 6).

2. What TikTok data is accessed

The integration accesses only:

  • Basic profile information for the authorized account: its TikTok open_id (an account identifier) and its display_name. This is read from TikTok's /v2/user/info endpoint and is used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video publishing: the ability to upload and publish video files that the operator supplies, to the authorized account itself, through TikTok's Content Posting API.

The integration does not access other users' profiles or content, followers, direct messages, comments, or viewing analytics, and it does not collect personal data from any third party. No TikTok data is read for advertising or profiling.

3. Why the data is used

Basic profile information is used solely to identify and confirm the connected account. The publishing capability is used solely to publish the operator's own video content to that account. No TikTok data is used for any other purpose.

4. Storage and retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored on PAOE's own servers in an encrypted secret store; they are encrypted at rest and are never printed in logs. They are retained only while the connection is active, so that scheduled publishing can continue, and are replaced when TikTok issues new tokens. Basic profile information is retained only as long as needed to identify the connected account. When the authorization is revoked or the connection is removed, the associated tokens are deleted. No TikTok data is retained for longer than the connection requires.

5. Sharing

TikTok data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is not used for advertising. The only parties involved are:

  • TikTok, which receives the API requests needed to identify the account and publish videos.
  • Infrastructure providers that host PAOE's servers, which may process data on PAOE's behalf under its instructions.

Data may be disclosed if required by law.

6. Deletion and revocation

You can revoke PAOE's access at any time from your TikTok account's Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions (or the equivalent TikTok settings screen), and remove PAOE from the list of connected apps. Once access is revoked, PAOE can no longer publish to the account and the stored tokens stop working. You may also request deletion of any data PAOE holds about the connection by contacting the address at the foot of this page; such requests are actioned promptly.

7. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest and masked in logs. Access to the integration is restricted to the operator. Credentials are never embedded in public code.

8. Children

This integration is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
